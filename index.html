The Swamp

SWAMPDOGE is a sports-culture-driven meme cryptocurrency built on Solana. It blends entertainment, competitive sports prediction, and decentralized community engagement into one ecosystem.

At its core is SwampBets — a prediction-based leaderboard where community members compete using their sports knowledge. Instead of just holding a meme token, users actively participate, earn recognition, and get rewarded for engagement.

The project aims to merge meme culture with daily interaction and reward-based participation, creating an ecosystem where competition, fun, and community drive long-term growth. The long-term goal is to establish SWAMPDOGE as the premier sports-themed meme token on Solana, powered by sustained engagement and recurring participation cycles.
